Questions

Photography booking FAQ.

Where is EyezLikeLenz based?

EyezLikeLenz is based at OMJ Studios in London. Travel within Greater London is included, and commissions elsewhere in the UK are available with travel quoted separately.

How much does a photography session cost?

Standard sessions cost £110 for up to two hours, £215 for up to four hours, or £525 for up to eight hours. Weddings, multi-day shoots and bespoke brand commissions are quoted individually.

How long does photo delivery take?

Private online galleries are delivered within 14 working days for standard sessions and within 28 days for full-day and wedding commissions. Wedding sneak peeks are supplied within 48 hours.

How do I reserve a date?

Choose a standard session through the online calendar, select an available date and complete payment. Weddings and bespoke commissions begin with an enquiry so the coverage and quote can be agreed first.

Are all photographs kept in one portfolio?

Yes. Weddings, events, portraits, editorial work and travel photographs remain together as one evolving EyezLikeLenz portfolio.
