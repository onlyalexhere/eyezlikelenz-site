01 / About

Be present.
I’ll keep the memories.

A calm presence behind the camera. An eye for the moments that matter.

EyezLikeLenz is a photography practice based in London and working across the UK. The work spans weddings, parties, fashion editorial, and brand campaigns, with a consistent attention to atmosphere, light, and feeling.

I’m Alex Oyekola, the London photographer behind EyezLikeLenz. My approach is documentary led, with gentle direction when you need it and room to be yourself when you don’t. From the quiet anticipation to the last dance, the aim is simple: photographs that feel like you.
