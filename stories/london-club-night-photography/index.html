Nightlife · London · Portfolio story

A London club night,
from booth to crowd.

A fast-moving night photographed as a complete atmosphere: the focus behind the booth, the room under red and amber light, and the guests who gave the event its energy.

The photography

People, atmosphere
and a sense of place.

The approach was to work lightly through the room and let moments build. Wide frames established the venue, closer portraits held the personality of the crowd, and the DJ sequence connected the whole story. Client names and private event details are intentionally omitted.
