The photographer

I’m Alex Oyekola.
This is EyezLikeLenz.

A London photographer drawn to people, atmosphere and the details that make a day feel like yours.

I photograph weddings, events, portraits and editorial commissions across London and the UK. EyezLikeLenz began with a simple aim: to make photographs that carry the feeling of being there, rather than forcing every moment into a pose.

My approach is documentary led and people first. I watch for the exchanges, expressions and details that can disappear in a busy room, then offer calm direction whenever it helps. The result is an honest set of images with shape, warmth and intention.

Every final photograph is selected and edited by me. Clients receive a private online gallery, with clear planning and communication from the first enquiry through to delivery.
